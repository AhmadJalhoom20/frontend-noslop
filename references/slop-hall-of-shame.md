# The Anti-Slop Hall of Shame

This document catalogs the 8 most egregious visual and architectural clichés generated by AI coding agents by default—and the exact architectural fixes required to eliminate them.

---

### 1. The Generic Purple-to-Cyan Gradient
* **The Tell:** `bg-gradient-to-r from-indigo-500 via-purple-500 to-pink-500` applied to headlines, borders, or glowing backdrop orbs.
* **Why It’s Slop:** It is the single most over-represented CSS snippet in modern training data. It immediately screams "unprompted AI template" rather than a thought-out brand palette.
* **The Fix:** Derive your color palette from the actual subject matter using **OKLCH color space**. A gradient is permitted only when it serves an optical hierarchy role (such as depth elevation or state change), never as generic filler.

---

### 2. The Cliché 3-Card Feature Grid
* **The Tell:** A centered heading followed by a uniform 3-column grid of rounded cards, each sporting a glowing border, a generic Lucide icon in a pastel square, and two lines of filler text.
* **Why It’s Slop:** Lazy information architecture. Most products don't have exactly three identical features that fit in identical boxes.
* **The Fix:** Asymmetrical layout design. Mix interactive code snippets, live previews, timeline flows, or data-dense comparison rows.

---

### 3. Hollow, Robot-Generated Marketing Copy
* **The Tell:** *"Unlock the power of next-generation AI workflows to streamline your synergy."*
* **Why It’s Slop:** High abstraction, zero concrete facts, buzzword soup.
* **The Fix:** Specific, human, and direct prose. State exactly what the tool does, who it is for, and how fast it runs. (Enforced via `no-ai-slop`).

---

### 4. Gratuitous Sparkle Buttons (`✨ Magic`)
* **The Tell:** Every primary button has a `✨` or `Sparkles` icon next to the text.
* **Why It’s Slop:** It trivializes AI interaction and dates the software to the 2023–2024 AI wrapper craze.
* **The Fix:** Clean, confident action verbs: *"Generate Report"*, *"Deploy to Production"*, *"Analyze Schema"*.

---

### 5. Floating Isolated Modals Without State Hierarchy
* **The Tell:** Blank screens while data is loading, followed by popups that have no animations or keyboard escape handlers.
* **Why It’s Slop:** Complete disregard for the 5 fundamental component states (Empty, Loading, Populated, Error, Disabled).
* **The Fix:** Skeleton screens that reserve layout dimensions, fluid physics-based transitions via `vaul` / `motion`, and full ARIA keyboard handling.

---

### 6. The "Tailwind Default" Radius & Shadow
* **The Tell:** `rounded-2xl shadow-xl border border-white/10 backdrop-blur-md` on every single surface.
* **Why It’s Slop:** Median styling from copy-pasted component libraries.
* **The Fix:** Cohesive geometry. If your typography is sharp and architectural, use sharp borders (`rounded-none` or `rounded-sm`) with crisp 1px borders. If your brand is organic, use intentional, calibrated radii.

---

### 7. Non-Semantic HTML & Ignored Accessibility
* **The Tell:** `div` tags with `onClick` handlers, missing `aria-*` attributes, un-navigable by keyboard.
* **Why It’s Slop:** Broken for screen readers and power users.
* **The Fix:** Semantic elements (`button`, `dialog`, `nav`, `main`) combined with unstyled primitives from **Radix UI** or **Base UI**.

---

### 8. React Re-render & Waterfall Bloat
* **The Tell:** Scattering `"use client"` at the root of Next.js pages, cascading `useEffect` data fetching loops, unmemoized heavy lists.
* **Why It’s Slop:** Sluggish frame rates and high Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).
* **The Fix:** Server Components by default, streaming Suspense boundaries, and zero-CLS skeleton reservations (Enforced via `react-best-practices`).
